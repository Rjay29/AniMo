Our mission

AniMo makes it easier to discover and support local agricultural producers while learning how to use ingredients that are regional, seasonal, and nutritious.

Our approach

We blend community sourcing, recipe inspiration, and transparent market information so people can shop with confidence and purpose.

Why it matters

Shorter supply chains, informed consumers, and farmer-led prices create healthier, more resilient local food ecosystems.
